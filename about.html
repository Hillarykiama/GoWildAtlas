Our Story

Born in the Bush.

GoWildAtlas was created to take wildlife medicine beyond the classroom and into the field. We connect veterinary professionals and wildlife enthusiasts with real conservation work across diverse ecosystems — from savannahs and deserts to rainforests and coastlines. Our immersive programmes are designed to turn knowledge into experience, and passion into real-world impact.

How It Started

GoWildAtlas.

What started as a single wildlife veterinary programme in South Africa has grown into a multi-country operation placing participants at the heart of real conservation work across Africa.

The name GoWildAtlas reflects what we’ve always believed — that this is more than travel. It’s a compass for people who want to do more than observe. To intervene. To contribute. To conserve.

Every programme we design is guided by one question: What would create the most meaningful field experience of a wildlife professional’s life?

What Drives Us

Three Words. One Global Mission.

◎

Explore

From Big Five reserves and desert landscapes to coastlines, marine ecosystems, and tropical rainforests — our programmes span the planet’s most extraordinary wildlife habitats. Each destination presents new species, new terrain, and new clinical realities. No two field experiences are ever the same.

✦

Intervene

This is not observation. This is participation. You work alongside wildlife veterinarians in the field — assisting immobilisations, health checks, monitoring projects, and real-time conservation operations. You become part of the team from day one.

⟳

Conserve

Every procedure, every dataset, every early morning and late night contributes to long-term conservation impact — from disease surveillance and population monitoring to ecosystem protection across continents. Your experience leaves a footprint that lasts far beyond your time in the field.

Who We Welcome

Built for Anyone Drawn to
Wildlife, Medicine & Field Conservation.

These programmes are not defined by where you are in your journey — but by where you want to go. Whether you're beginning, transitioning, or already working in the field, this is your entry into real wildlife conservation environments.

You will not be observing from the sidelines. You will be working alongside veterinary and conservation teams in active field operations across Africa.

  • ▸
    Veterinary Nurses & Technicians

  • ▸
    Qualified Veterinarians

  • ▸

  • ▸
    Career Changers

No matter your background, what matters most is curiosity, commitment, and willingness to step into real conservation work.
