What to Pack for a Wildlife Conservation Programme

Bring less than you think, but make the essentials count.

GoWildAtlasMay 20, 20263 min read
Back to Blog

Pack for long days, changing temperatures and dust. Neutral breathable clothing, a warm layer, a waterproof shell, sturdy boots, a hat, sunscreen, insect repellent and a small notebook will do more for you than almost anything fancy.

Think Field Practical

Field teams move quickly, laundry can be limited and the best kit is the gear you can wear, clean, carry and repair without drama. Avoid bright colours, heavy luggage and anything you would be heartbroken to stain.

Your programme team will send a destination-specific packing list before travel, but the principle is the same everywhere: comfortable, durable, simple.
