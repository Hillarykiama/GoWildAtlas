Why Volunteers Pay and What Programme Fees Support

Safe, ethical field experiences need real infrastructure.

GoWildAtlasMay 20, 20264 min read
Back to Blog

Programme fees support accommodation, meals, field transport, airport transfers, supervision, equipment access and the logistics needed to place participants safely inside conservation work.

More Than a Bed and a Vehicle

Good programmes require planning, staff time, local partnerships, emergency readiness and a structure that protects both participants and wildlife.

Transparency matters. Participants should understand what is included, what is not included and how their presence fits into the work on the ground.
