From Classroom to Field: Preparing for a Veterinary Gap Year

A conservation gap year can build confidence, perspective and clearer career direction before formal training or clinical placements.

Go With Intention

The strongest experiences come from curiosity and commitment. Ask questions, keep notes and reflect on what you are learning about animals, people and yourself.

You do not need to arrive as an expert. You do need to arrive ready to listen, work and adapt.
