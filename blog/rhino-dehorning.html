Does Dehorning Help Protect Rhinos?

It is one tool, not a complete solution.

GoWildAtlasMay 20, 20263 min read
Back to Blog

Rhino dehorning is used in some conservation settings to reduce poaching risk, but it only works as part of a wider protection strategy.

A Wider System

Security, monitoring, habitat management, veterinary care and community engagement all matter. Dehorning can reduce incentive, but it cannot replace long-term conservation infrastructure.

For participants, observing this work helps reveal the difficult practical decisions behind protecting endangered species.
