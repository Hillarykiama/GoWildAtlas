Who We Are

Wildlife medicine,
lived in the field.

GoWildAtlas gives you the opportunity to step inside real conservation projects across Africa. Work alongside experienced conservation professionals, contribute to meaningful field activities, and experience firsthand how wildlife and wild places are protected every day.

Whether you're passionate about wildlife, conservation, marine environments, or simply looking for an unforgettable volunteer experience, this is where curiosity becomes knowledge, and adventure gains purpose. No staged experiences. No tourist attractions. Just authentic conservation in the wild.
