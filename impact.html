What Impact Means Here

Not volunteer tourism. Useful hands, careful teaching, and partner-led conservation work.

Impact begins before a participant arrives. Every programme is designed with local conservation teams so the work fits genuine field needs, from clinical support and monitoring to documentation, husbandry and community outreach.

Participants gain rare exposure to wildlife medicine and conservation operations, while partners gain additional capacity for work that is time-sensitive, labour-intensive and often under-resourced.

How We Measure

Three lenses for honest reporting

We look at contribution from the field outward: what animals receive, what partners can do more effectively, and what participants carry into future conservation or veterinary careers.

  • Protocols, records and repeatable workflows

Field Ethics Pledge

What we commit to. Every programme, every season.

These are not aspirations. They are operating conditions. If we can't meet them at a given partner site, we don't run the programme there.

Welfare before learning

Animal welfare is never compromised to create a learning moment. If an intervention is stressful for an animal beyond what the procedure requires, participants step back.

Partner-led, always

No task is initiated by GoWildAtlas staff. The conservation or veterinary team leads. We follow. Participants follow us. The hierarchy of expertise is non-negotiable.

No handling without supervision

Participants do not handle wildlife independently at any point. Every physical interaction with an animal happens alongside a qualified professional who has cleared it.

Honest group sizes

Groups are capped at 6–8 participants. We do not scale up to increase revenue. Larger groups compromise supervision quality and field safety.

No manufactured experiences

We do not create procedures for the sake of participant engagement. If a health check or intervention wasn't already planned by the field team, it doesn't happen because volunteers are present.

Fair compensation to partners

Partner sites receive meaningful financial support from every placement. We don't use conservation as a backdrop and return nothing to the people doing the actual work.

Honest programme descriptions

If a programme involves more observation than hands-on work in a given season, we say so before you book. You will not arrive expecting to dart rhinos and spend a week watching from a distance with no warning.

No trophy, canned or captive hunting adjacent operations

None of our partner sites support or benefit from trophy hunting, canned hunting, or lion bone trade. We vet partners on this explicitly.
